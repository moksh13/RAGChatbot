Category: Investor Services - Statements & Tax Documents
How to Download Capital Gains Statement from HDFC Mutual Fund Official Website:
1. Visit the official HDFC Mutual Fund investor portal: https://www.hdfcfund.com/investor-services/statements.
2. Navigate to 'Investor Services' and click on 'Statements & Certificates'.
3. Select 'Capital Gain Statement' or 'Account Statement'.
4. Enter your registered Folio Number or PAN and authenticate via One-Time Password (OTP) sent to your registered mobile/email.
5. Select the relevant Financial Year and download the statement as PDF or Excel.

How to Download Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) via CAMS Portal:
1. Go to CAMS Online (www.camsonline.com) -> 'Investor Services' -> 'Statements' -> 'Capital Gains / Consolidated Account Statement (CAS)'.
2. Provide your registered email address, PAN, and select statement period (e.g., Specific Financial Year).
3. Submit the request. A password-protected PDF statement is automatically emailed to your registered email address.

How to Download Capital Gains Statement on Groww:
1. Open Groww web or mobile app and click on your Profile picture.
2. Navigate to 'Reports' section.
3. Select 'Capital Gains Statement' and choose 'Mutual Funds'.
4. Select the desired financial year and click 'Download PDF' or 'Send to Email'.

Important Notes on Statements:
- Capital gains statements clearly segregate Short Term Capital Gains (STCG) and Long Term Capital Gains (LTCG) for income tax filing.
- Statements are encrypted with a password (typically your PAN in uppercase or date of birth in DDMMYYYY format).
